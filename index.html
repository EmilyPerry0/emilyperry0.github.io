Emily Perry

My name is Emily Perry and I am currently a master's student at the Univeristy of Amsterdam. I am studying Software Engineering until the fall of 2026. I am currently working on my thesis project, which is called "Re-Engineering a Robotic Imiatation Learning Pipeline and Integrating a VLM as the Reward System." More information can be found on the Master Thesis page.

I moved to Amsterdam after growing up in Texas and attending the Colorado School of Mines, where I received my BSc in Computer Science with a focus in robotics and intelligent systems, graduating magna cum laude. I have a passion for everything computer software related, and am especially interested in how this intersects with robotics and space. I have worked on various robotics projects in my academic career, such as my thesis, reinvigorating and improving a robotic art installation, and automating a maze-solving robot with model-based design.
